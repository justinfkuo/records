Official USATF

USATF Statistics & Official Records

The primary USA Track & Field statistics portal at usatf.org/resources/statistics publishes ratified records alongside national champions, season top lists, and historical data.

  • • American Records Database: Filterable database and printer-friendly lists for Open & U20 (Indoor & Outdoor) and U.S. All-Comers records.
  • • World & Championship Meet Records: Official World records and USATF National Championship meet marks.
  • • Statistics & Tools: USATF Champions, Season Top Lists, Team USA Medal Tables, Annual Awards, Calculators, and Record Applications.

Para T&F

USATF Paralympic Track & Field Records

U.S. Paralympic (Para) Track & Field records are maintained separately from the main USATF website on the dedicated U.S. Paralympics Track & Field portal (usparatf.org) and by Adaptive Track & Field USA.

  • • USATF Para National Records (usparatf.org/records): Interactive national records database by sport class, Track & Field record application forms, ratification rules, and links to World Para Athletics (WPA) Americas & World records.
  • • Adaptive Track & Field USA (atfusa.org): Maintains U.S. Junior (U11, U14, U17, U20), Masters (M35, M50, M60), and Open adaptive track & field records under WPA and ATFUSA rules.

LDR Records

Long Distance Road Running Records

U.S. road running and long-distance track records maintained by Andy Carr are hosted at usatfldrrecords.org (the USATF Long Distance Running Records database). Look up American Open, Junior, and Masters age-group marks as well as record application forms and verification criteria.

  • • Search by Event: Filter separately for Road or Track distances.
  • • Search by Age Group: Look up age-specific records for both Road and Track events.
  • • Search by Name: Look up specific marks held by an individual Athlete or Club.
Visit usatfldrrecords.org
Masters T&F

Masters Track & Field Records

U.S. Masters Track & Field records are maintained separately from the main USATF site on the USATF Masters website, with a dedicated lookup database at masterstrackandfield.org.

  • • USATF Masters Hub (usatfmasters.org/records/): Maintained by the USATF Masters Track & Field (MTF) committee. Houses links to American and World Masters Indoor, Outdoor, and Race Walk records (5-year age brackets starting at age 35), application forms, and ratification statuses.
  • • USA Masters TF Record Lookup (masterstrackandfield.org): Search Indoor and Outdoor marks by Event, Age Group, Athlete, or Club. Note: Accurate through 1/1/2023
